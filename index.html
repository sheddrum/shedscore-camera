ShedScore

Phone camera

Shows this phone’s camera full screen with no buttons, so ShedScore on your Mac can record it through the cable.

  1. Connect this phone to your Mac with a cable.
  2. Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock and hold the phone sideways.
  3. Start the camera below.
  4. In ShedScore, choose “iPhone (USB screen mirror)” as the camera.
Picture

For a picture with no Safari bars, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”, and open this page from its new icon.

The picture stays on this phone and travels to your Mac through the cable. Nothing is uploaded.
